# Eki Studio: the team guide

Five words at the top of the screen are all you need.

| Word | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | What needs you, what is moving, how the week is going |
| **Make** | Turn one idea into a reel or a carousel |
| **Review** | Watch it, fix the caption, approve it or ask for changes |
| **People** | Everyone who messaged you, and what Eki said back |
| **Autopilot** | Six jobs Eki does on its own, each with one switch |

## Start here: one minute

Open the account menu (top right) and choose **How Eki works**, then press **Start the demo**. It runs the real product on sample data: an idea becomes a reel, the reel gets approved and posted, then a customer gets answered.

## Practice mode

You begin in Practice mode, shown by the striped band at the top. Everything really runs, but nothing leaves Eki: no posts, no messages, no charges. Practise as much as you like. The checklist at the bottom of the screen ticks itself as you try each thing.

## Your day

1. Open **Today**. The headline tells you how many things need you.
2. Press **Start reviewing**. Watch each piece and press **Approve and post**, **Post later** or **Ask for changes**. On a computer, the **A** key approves and **R** asks for changes.
3. Have an idea? Type it into the box on Today and press **Make it**. You can close the page. It will be waiting in Review.

## Going live

Open **Go live** at the top right and connect your accounts one at a time. Paste the key and press **Save and check**. Eki tests it straight away and tells you in plain words if something is wrong.

| What you connect | What it unlocks | Where you get it |
|---|---|---|
| Your voice (brand details) | Everything sounds like you | You write it |
| AI writer: OpenAI, Groq or CodeCraft (pick one) | Scripts, carousels, posts | Their developer site |
| ElevenLabs | Voices for reels | elevenlabs.io |
| Runway | Filming for reels | runwayml.com |
| Instagram and Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Buffer | Posting | Each platform's developer site |
| WhatsApp | Replies and follow-ups | Meta Business |
| Telegram | Approving from your phone (optional) | @BotFather |
| Resend | Emails and the Monday report | resend.com |

Carousels need only the AI writer. Reels also need ElevenLabs and Runway. Add the rest whenever you like.

Going live deletes the sample content, switches every autopilot off so you can choose again, and from then on what you approve is really posted and providers really charge you.

## If something goes wrong

- **A piece says it stopped.** Open it. It says why, in plain words, and has a **Try again** button. Nothing half-finished is ever posted.
- **A connection says Not working.** Open **Go live**, read the message, fix the key and press **Save and check** again.
- **You need everything to stop right now.** Open **Autopilot** and press **Stop everything**. No messages or posts go out until an owner starts them again.
- **Today says the studio is offline.** That is a technical fix. Send a screenshot to your developer.

## The back office

Account menu > **Back office** has the detail: run history, costs, full brand settings, workflow versions, team members. Day to day you should not need it.
