# Eki Content Pillars & Video Script Strategy

Eki is a digital marketplace connecting African foodstuff vendors and exporters with local and international buyers (such as the African diaspora and global restaurateurs in Italy and worldwide). This document outlines our content foundations, hooks library, video script templates, and referral campaign mechanics.

---

## The 5 Content Pillars of Eki

To capture and retain our diverse target audiences (domestic vendors, exporters, and international buyers), we distribute all organic social media posts across 5 pillars.

*   **Target Channels**: TikTok (relatable comedy skits), Instagram (carousel diagrams of current broken loops), LinkedIn (industry write-ups on export risks).
*   **Hashtag Core**: `#SourcingAfricanFood #ExportNigeria #AfricanDiasporaItaly #TrustedVendor`

## Launch Referral Campaigns

Workflows will track these campaigns to incentivize user signups.

### 1. "Skip the Waitlist" VIP Pass (Milestone: 3 Referrals)
*   **Mechanic**: When a waitlisted user refers 3 verified friends, they get bumped to the "Beta Tester" cohort, granting them early access to Eki's platform 2 weeks before official launch.
*   **Emails Triggered**: Sent via Resend on 3 successful signups.

### 2. "Free Escrow Transacting" (Milestone: 5 Referrals)
*   **Mechanic**: For every 5 successful referrals, vendors unlock 3 months of 0% transaction fees on Eki's escrow payment gateway (saving up to $250 in transaction fees).
*   **Target Audience**: Exporters and local vendors.
